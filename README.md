# Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project showcases a **comprehensive Power BI dashboard** developed for **Sales Performance Analysis**, focused on monitoring **sales revenue, order trends, customer performance, product demand, category insights, and regional sales distribution** across major Indian cities.

The dashboard is designed to support **data-driven decision-making** for sales managers, business analysts, and leadership teams by providing a consolidated, interactive view of critical sales KPIs.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Track **total sales, quantity sold, and total orders**
* Monitor **sales trends over time** against defined targets
* Analyze **top customers by sales contribution**
* Evaluate **product and category-wise order performance**
* Understand **regional sales distribution and order status**
* Monitor **operational efficiency through order lifecycle tracking**

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### 1️⃣ Sales & Revenue Analysis

* Overall sales performance tracked against predefined targets  
* Sales trend over time indicates consistent goal achievement  
* Category-wise sales distribution highlights **Electronics** as the leading revenue contributor  

---

### 2️⃣ Customer Performance Analysis

* **Top 10 customers by total sales** identified  
* Sneha Gupta and Rajesh Kumar emerge as the highest revenue-generating customers  
* Enables customer segmentation and targeted sales strategies  

---

### 3️⃣ Product & Category Insights

* **Top 5 products by order volume** visualized using a waterfall chart  
* Electronics and Accessories dominate order counts  
* Identifies low-performing product categories for optimization  

---

### 4️⃣ Order Status & Operational Insights

* Order distribution across **Completed, Pending, On Process, and Cancelled** statuses  
* Majority of orders are completed, indicating healthy operational performance  
* Daily order trends highlight peak ordering days  

---

### 5️⃣ Regional Performance Analysis

* City-wise analysis for **Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Mumbai, and Pune**  
* Geographic map visualization displays sales and order status distribution  
* Helps identify high-performing and underperforming regions  

---

### 6️⃣ Interactive Filtering

* Filters for **Region and Order Status**  
* Cross-filtering enabled across all visuals  
* Drill-down capability for detailed performance insights
